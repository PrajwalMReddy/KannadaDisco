## Google Play

**App name** [30]: KannadaDisco

**Short description** [80]:
Learn Kannada with lessons, vocabulary, audio and a daily word.

**Full description** [4000]:

KannadaDisco teaches Kannada simply and clearly, whether you're moving to
Karnataka, already live there, or love Kannada culture.

LEARN STEP BY STEP
• 20 course lessons, from vowels and consonants to tenses and case endings
• Reading and pronunciation: vowels, consonants, conjuncts, digits and time
• Vocabulary: family, food, animals, colours, verbs and more
• Grammar tables for nouns, pronouns, tenses and suffixes
• Conversations for real situations: the market, a cab, a restaurant,
  government offices and more

HEAR EVERY WORD
Tap any word to hear it spoken in Kannada. Every word also comes with its
transliteration and English meaning.

WORD OF THE DAY
Learn a new word every day, with a daily reminder at a time you choose.

KIDS SECTION
Picture vocabulary and Kannada rhymes with videos for young learners.

No accounts, no ads and no tracking.

**What's new** [500]:
• 20 new course lessons
• Choose when your daily Word of the Day reminder arrives
• Word of the Day now changes reliably every day
• Spelling fixes and smoother performance
