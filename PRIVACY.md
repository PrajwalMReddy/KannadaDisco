# KannadaDisco Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

KannadaDisco is a free app for learning Kannada, made by Prajwal M Reddy.
This policy covers the KannadaDisco apps on Google Play and the App Store.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** KannadaDisco has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal
information.

## What stays on your device

The app saves a few settings on your phone so it works the way you left it:

- the time you chose for the daily Word of the Day reminder
- which Word of the Day you are on, and the date it last changed

These never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to remind you about the Word of the Day once a day.
  You can turn this off at any time in your phone's settings.
- **Internet**: to load the rhyme videos in the Kids section.

## Text-to-speech

When you tap a word to hear it, the app uses the text-to-speech voice built
into your phone. KannadaDisco doesn't send the text anywhere itself.

## YouTube videos

The rhymes in the Kids section play videos from YouTube inside the app.
When you play a video, YouTube provides it, and
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to
what YouTube collects. KannadaDisco does not receive any of that information.

## Children

KannadaDisco has a section for children. Because the app collects no
personal information, it collects none from children either.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
